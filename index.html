About Me

Data rarely gives you the answer directly.

It gives you clues.

I’m a Mathematics graduate from Universitas Sebelas Maret who enjoys connecting those clues — finding patterns, questioning assumptions, and turning raw data into insights that can actually be used.

My work spans Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI, with projects covering customer analytics, profitability, predictive modeling, business performance, and public data.

I’m less interested in making data look complicated than making complicated problems easier to understand.

That’s where the analysis begins.

My Projects

Maven Toys – Sales Performance & Expansion Strategy

Analyzed retail sales, product, store, and inventory data to evaluate revenue performance, profitability, product performance, and store productivity. Built a relational data model and developed DAX measures to identify growth trends, inventory stockouts, and potential expansion signals across locations. The analysis found $14.44M in revenue, $4.01M in gross profit, and a 27.79% gross margin, while highlighting location and inventory factors relevant to expansion decisions.

Customer Analytics – RFM, Cohort & Market Basket Analysis

Analyzed customer transaction data to understand customer value, retention behavior, and purchasing patterns. Applied RFM segmentation, cohort analysis, and market basket analysis using Python to identify high-value customers, inactive customer segments, retention patterns, and frequently purchased product combinations. Credit Card Default Prediction

Developed machine learning models to predict credit card default risk using customer demographics and historical financial behavior. Performed feature engineering, handled class imbalance, and compared Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost models using recall-focused evaluation. Implemented GridSearchCV and out-of-time validation across different observation horizons to evaluate the model's ability to identify customers at risk of default.

Analyzed public data on access to drinking water across 20 districts and 208 villages in Kabupaten Sragen from 2021 to 2024. Used Excel to clean, standardize, compare, and summarize the data, then applied QGIS to visualize geographic patterns and differences in drinking water access. The project demonstrates how public data and spatial analysis can be combined to identify geographic disparities and support data-driven evaluation.

Superstore Sales & Profitability Analytics

Analyzed 9,994 retail transactions to evaluate sales performance, profitability, product performance, customer segments, regional performance, and discount impact. Combined Python-based exploratory analysis with an interactive Excel dashboard to identify key profitability gaps, including Furniture generating only around a 2% margin and Texas generating approximately $170K in sales while incurring a $25.7K loss. The analysis also explored the relationship between discount levels and declining margins to identify opportunities for more profitable growth.
